Elevate Pumpkin Drive

Official Contest Rules

Last updated September 22, 2026

No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or win.

1Sponsor

Elevate Realty Group, brokered by Real Broker, Edmonton, Alberta (the "Sponsor").

Contact: Brent Anderson, Brent@elevaterealtygroup.ca, (780) 297-6727

2Eligibility

Open to legal residents of Alberta who are 18 years of age or older at the time of entry.

The following are not eligible to enter or win: agents, employees and contractors of Elevate Realty Group and Real Broker, and members of their immediate families or households.

Residents of Quebec are not eligible.

3Contest Period

The contest opens September 22, 2026 and closes at 11:59 PM Mountain Time on November 2, 2026 (the "Contest Period").

Entries received outside the Contest Period will not be counted.

4How to Enter

There are two ways to receive entries. Both are free.

Method A: Accept a pumpkin (1 entry)

Request a free pumpkin by October 2, 2026 through the request form or by replying to the invitation email or text. Each household that requests a pumpkin and is present or reachable for delivery receives one (1) entry.

Pumpkins are limited to two (2) per household and are subject to availability. Limit one (1) Method A entry per household regardless of how many pumpkins are requested or delivered. Requesting two pumpkins does not produce two entries.

Method B: Post a carved pumpkin photo (2 additional entries)

Between October 29 and November 2, 2026, post a photograph of your carved pumpkin to a public Instagram, Facebook or TikTok account and tag @elevaterealty.yeg. Qualifying posts receive two (2) additional entries.

To qualify, the post must be publicly visible, must show a carved pumpkin, and must tag the Sponsor. Private accounts cannot be verified and will not be counted.

Limit one (1) Method B entry set per household. Maximum three (3) total entries per household.

Free alternate method of entry

You may enter without accepting a pumpkin or posting a photo. Send an email to Brent@elevaterealtygroup.ca with the subject line "Pumpkin Contest Entry" and include your full name, mailing address, phone number and email address. One entry per household. Must be received by 11:59 PM Mountain Time on November 2, 2026.

5Prize

One (1) prize consisting of two (2) lower bowl tickets to the Edmonton Oilers versus Vegas Golden Knights game at Rogers Place on Tuesday, November 24, 2026.

Approximate retail value: $480.00 CAD.

The prize is awarded as is, with no cash equivalent and no substitution. Tickets will be transferred electronically. The winner is responsible for transportation, parking, food, beverages and all other costs not expressly included.

If the game is postponed, rescheduled or cancelled for any reason, the Sponsor's obligation is limited to the tickets as issued and the Sponsor is not responsible for any resulting change in value or usability.

6Odds of Winning

Odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible entries received during the Contest Period.

7Draw

The draw will take place on November 3, 2026 in Edmonton, Alberta. Each eligible entry will be assigned a sequential number and the winning number will be selected by random number generator. The draw will be recorded.

8Skill Testing Question

Before being declared a winner, the selected entrant must correctly answer, without assistance of any kind, a time limited mathematical skill testing question administered by phone or email.

9Notification and Claim

The selected entrant will be contacted by phone and email using the information provided at entry.

If the selected entrant cannot be reached within three (3) business days, declines the prize, fails the skill testing question, or is found to be ineligible, the prize will be forfeited and a new entrant will be drawn.

The winner may be required to sign a declaration and release before the prize is awarded.

10Publicity and Photo Release

By posting a photo and tagging the Sponsor under Method B, you grant Elevate Realty Group a non exclusive, royalty free licence to reproduce, display and share that photo on its social media accounts, website and marketing materials, with credit to your account handle.

By accepting the prize, the winner consents to the use of their name, city and photograph for promotional purposes without further compensation.

You may withdraw consent for the use of your photo at any time by emailing Brent@elevaterealtygroup.ca.

11Privacy

Personal information collected is used only to administer this contest and the pumpkin delivery. It will not be sold. It may be retained in the Sponsor's client database in accordance with applicable privacy legislation and existing business relationships. You may request removal at any time by emailing Brent@elevaterealtygroup.ca.

12General Conditions

The Sponsor reserves the right to disqualify any entry that is fraudulent, duplicated, or does not comply with these rules.

The Sponsor reserves the right to cancel, suspend or amend this contest if it cannot be conducted as planned, subject to any required regulatory approval.

This contest is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram, Meta, TikTok, the Edmonton Oilers, Oilers Entertainment Group or the National Hockey League. By entering, you release those parties from any liability arising from this contest.

All decisions of the Sponsor are final.
